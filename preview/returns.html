Customer Protection

DEFECT & REPLACEMENT POLICY

Honest transparency regarding returns and product guarantees.

What We Cover

At AltrixWear, we stand behind the craftsmanship and quality of our athletic and compression apparel. We offer 100% free replacements or full refunds for:

  • Seller-side errors: Incorrect size, color, or wrong item sent.
  • Damaged or defective goods: Rips, faulty zippers, seam tears, or material flaws present upon arrival.

Change-of-Mind Policy

Due to the intimate, skin-contact nature of performance compression wear and shapewear, as well as our direct-to-consumer pricing structure, we do not accept change-of-mind returns or discretionary exchanges once an item has been delivered.

We strongly advise consulting our detailed Size Guide before purchasing. If you are ever uncertain between two sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for comfortable everyday compression.

How to File a Claim

If your item arrived damaged or you received an incorrect item, please email support@altrixwear.com within 7 calendar days of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the issue. Our support team will issue a replacement or refund immediately.
