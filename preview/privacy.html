PRIVACY POLICY

How AltrixWear safeguards your personal information and online data.

At ALTRIXWEAR, accessible from altrixwear.com, one of our primary priorities is the privacy of our visitors and customers. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal data we collect, how it is used, and the security protocols we employ to protect it.

Information We Collect

When you purchase activewear or shapewear from our store, we collect personal information you give us, such as your name, shipping address, billing address, email address, and phone number. This information is strictly utilized to process transactions, fulfill deliveries via USPS, and provide tracking updates.

Payment Security & Encryption

We never store, see, or process complete credit or debit card numbers on our servers. All transactions are encrypted and processed through industry-standard Level 1 PCI-DSS certified payment processors (Shopify Payments, Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay).

Third-Party Services

In general, the third-party providers used by us (such as shipping carriers and logistics fulfillment partners) will only collect, use, and disclose your information to the extent necessary to allow them to perform the services they provide to us.

Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have questions about our Privacy Policy or would like to request deletion of your personal data, please contact our data compliance officer at privacy@altrixwear.com.
