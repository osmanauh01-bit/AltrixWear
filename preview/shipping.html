SHIPPING POLICY

Transparent delivery timelines across the United States.

Domestic US Timeline Overview

  • Order Processing Time: 2 – 4 business days (quality check, sorting, and dispatch).
  • Total Estimated Delivery: 8 – 12 business days total from order confirmation to your doorstep.
  • Free Shipping: Automatically applied to all orders totaling $75 or more. Flat $4.99 standard shipping on orders below $75.

Tracking Your Order

Once your package leaves our fulfillment center, you will immediately receive an automated confirmation email containing your tracking number (USPS / DHL eCommerce). Please allow 24–48 hours for tracking portals to update.

Address Changes

If you need to adjust your delivery address, please contact support@altrixwear.com within 12 hours of placing your order. Once fulfillment has commenced, addresses cannot be modified.
